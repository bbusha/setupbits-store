Shipping & Delivery

Dispatch: orders are sent to our supplier within 1–3 business days. The supplier ships directly to you from their warehouse.

Delivery time: 7–14 business days from order date for US addresses. International orders may take longer; customs fees (if any) are the buyer's responsibility.

Tracking: a tracking link is emailed as soon as dispatch happens (usually within 2–3 days of purchase).

Shipping cost: included in the item price — no surcharge at checkout.

We don't control the carrier. If a parcel stalls in transit 10+ days beyond the estimate, contact us and we'll chase the supplier/carrier or replace/refund under the Returns policy.

Returns & Refunds

Window: 30 days from delivery date.

Eligible: items that arrive damaged, defective, wrong item, or not as described. Also accepted: unopened items you no longer want.

  • Damaged / wrong item: photograph the packaging and item and email us within 3 days of delivery — we ship a replacement or refund your payment, no arguing.
  • Unwanted item: write to us within 30 days for a return address and instructions (return shipping is paid by the buyer unless we sent the wrong thing).
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of the return being processed.

Lost parcels: if tracking shows delivered but the parcel is absent, contact us within 14 days — after carrier investigation we replace or refund.
